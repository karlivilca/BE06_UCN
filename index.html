2. Reading: Business Memos

Memo 1: Office Equipment

TO: All Staff

FROM: IT Department

DATE: March 15, 2024

SUBJECT: New Printer Instructions

We have a new color printer in the reception area on the first floor. Please follow these instructions: 1. Turn on the printer and wait for the green light. 2. Place your document face-down on the glass. 3. Select color or black-and-white printing. 4. Press start and collect your copies.

Please contact the IT department at extension 301 if you have problems.

Memo 2: Company Meeting Schedule

TO: All Employees

FROM: CEO Office

DATE: March 20, 2024

SUBJECT: Quarterly Business Meeting

Our quarterly company meeting is scheduled for Friday, March 22 at 10:00 AM in the main conference room. All staff members are required to attend. Please bring your quarterly reports. Light refreshments will be provided. The meeting will last approximately 2 hours.

Memo 3: Safety Notice

TO: All Employees

FROM: Security Department

DATE: March 25, 2024

SUBJECT: Emergency Procedures Reminder

Please remember these important safety instructions. Fire Emergency: Do not use the elevator. Use the stairs and meet at the assembly point in the parking lot. Call 911 if needed. Office Security: Always carry your access card. Do not let strangers into the building. Report any suspicious activity to security.
